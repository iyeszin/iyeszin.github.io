der Fluss entlang or den Fluss entlang?

The preposition "entlang" can be used with either the genitive or the dative case.

"Der Fluss" (the river) is a masculine noun. In the dative case, "der Fluss" becomes "dem Fluss." Therefore, both "entlang des Flusses" (genitive case) and "dem Fluss entlang" (dative case) are correct. "Der Fluss entlang" is incorrect because it does not follow the correct case usage.
