A1 - Food and drinks In Malay

← Back to Main: Malay for adults framework

Jom makan!

Food and drinks are an integral part of any culture, and Malaysian cuisine is no exception. Whether you're traveling to Malaysia or simply want to explore its rich culinary traditions, learning the vocabulary for food and drinks is essential. In this post, we'll cover basic terms for common foods and beverages, as well as useful phrases for ordering at restaurants. Mastering these words and phrases will help you navigate menus and communicate effectively in Malay.

Drinks | Minuman

English Malay
Tea Teh
Coffee Kopi
Milk Susu
Water Air
Juice Jus

Foods | Makanan

English Malay

Rice Nasi

Pork Daging Babi
Fish Ikan
Prawn Udang
Egg Telur

Useful Phrases

  • Please: Tolong
  • Thank you: Terima kasih
  • I do not eat...: Saya tidak makan ...
  • I want...: Saya mahu ...
  • One (piece/cup) of...: Satu ...
  • How much is this?: Berapa harga ini?
  • Can I have the bill, please?: Boleh saya minta bil, tolong?

Preserving Cultural Authenticity

I believe that using the original names for foods, such as nasi lemak and bak ku teh, helps preserve the cultural authenticity and precision of the dish. It also encourages people to learn more about the cuisine and its history. In an upcoming post, I will introduce the delicacies of local Malay food and explore their linguistic roots.

Learning the language of food is not just about communication—it's about connecting with a culture and its people. Whether you're ordering a teh tarik or enjoying a plate of nasi goreng, these phrases and vocabulary will enhance your experience and deepen your appreciation for the cuisine.

Selamat makan! (Enjoy your meal!)
